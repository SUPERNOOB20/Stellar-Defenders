Q: Is this a VN?
A: Good question, good question... I initially just chose VN format cuz I wanted to start with something easy - python was quite my field, RenPy is one
of the easiest engines ever, and 2 + 2 is 4.
But I also didn't want to make a +18 or romantic game... so that kinda set the tradeoff: I had to give in to VN cliches to some extent in exchange for
an environment that would make the coding part way easier ":3
Eventually I deciding on making the romantic part in postgame. That would not only make the most sense, but also affect my game the least :3

Q: Why does X region have X name? Where did "Irisium" come from...??
A: This game has a lot of symbolism. Also inspiration from greek and latin...
Regions have a pseudo-latin name. That is, they are based off of latin names for their corresponding colours, but are actually made up, fictional names... ":3
As for Irisium, well... IRISium... where all colours are born in human retinae... ;3

Q: Why does X character have X name?
A: Oh, ho-ho... interesting one. I honestly just parted from my base idea of the concepts of each region and character.
Then I considered a bunch of names until I got to ones that matched the corresponding theme and didn't sound bad!
- Phoebe, Cybele, Ariadne, and Rhea all come from greek mythology, if it wasn't already obvious...
	* Something cool about Phoebe is that I intentionally chose it for the benefit of having a protagonist with an unisex name.
I mean, it doesn't [i]need[/i] to be a unisex name, but when it comes to VNs it might be a good game design idea to "help the player relate to the protagonist".
So here we are...
- Hector comes from a joke from a video of an iconic chilean youtuber, German. Something something about a 3-meter-tall surfer with a huge D.
I thought it did kinda match the Central American region and the beach theme, so I gave it a go! "^^
- Amaru is just a name that matches his region's culture :3
	* Chun Min-Min is similar on this note, but with the distinction that it's a pseudo name - a fictional name that is just "based"
off of his region's typical names. I wanted something easy to remember, not a complex name. Surnames make for complex, hard to remember names...
so I thought repeating the surname was a good idea in this regard - Min-Min is easy to remember!!! Hmmm... now that I think of it...
maybe Chun Min was even easier to remember... or just Chun, in that matter.
But then lowly vagrant employees would mock him as "Mr. C", and he is no Cock nor Chicken! (# > .<)
so maybe plain Chun Min-Min suits him best, after all... (endless cycle @_@)
- As for Noon... well... there's nothing hotter than noon, is there! (ha)
