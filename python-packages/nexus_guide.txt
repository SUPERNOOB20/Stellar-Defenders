Q: What is The Nexus?
A: "nexus.txt" is just a plain text file, akin to "settings" files
in other games, such as JSON, .cfg, or .ini files.

Q: What purpose does The Nexus fulfill?
A: My game contains two main components:
world_map_handler.py and the RenPy project.

These two components are not compatible one another.
They need a way to communicate between themselves...

The Nexus stores persistent game data for these two components
to be able to share data with each other - hence, why it's called
The Nexus :)

Q: Can I tinker with The Nexus?
A: Only if you know what you're doing - it's akin to meddling with your
own save files, after all. If you lose your game progress for touching
things in wrong ways, I won't hold any responsibility for that :p
